    Written just now from the lecture's captions · 6 slides

    A quadratic is solved with Sridharacharya's formula. The discriminant tells you what kind of roots to expect.

    Takeaways

    • Standard form needs ; a, b, c are the coefficients.
    • The roots come from completing the square.
    • The sign of Δ decides: two real, one repeated, or complex roots.
